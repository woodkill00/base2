# Bounded antivirus startup assurance

Cold preview initialization must get its declared startup budget without becoming
available before antivirus readiness. Startup failure must retain a precise,
nonsecret reason before teardown. Failures remain closed, the preview lease stays
bounded, and automated acceptance cannot pass when its dependencies are unavailable.

No antivirus bypass, freshness relaxation, resource-size expansion, real mail,
interactive Google sign-in or design changes. The preceding trial's exact cause
is unknown; improved evidence must not be presented as retroactive proof.
